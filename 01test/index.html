Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the ability to use digital technologies and the internet safely, responsibly, ethically, and effectively.

What is Digital Law?

Digital Law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology, the internet, and digital information.

What is AUP?

An AUP is a set of rules that explains how people are allowed to use technology, computers, networks, and the internet in a school, workplace, or organization.

What is Plagiarism?

Plagiarism is using someone else's work or ideas without giving them credit.

Plagiarism is when you use someone else's words, ideas, images, or work and claim them as your own without giving proper credit.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is when a student cheats, lies, or uses unfair methods to gain an advantage in school.

Academic dishonesty is cheating or being dishonest in schoolwork or academic activities.

What are the rules for AI Generated Content?

AI-generated content rules are guidelines that help people use AI safely, honestly, responsibly, and ethically.

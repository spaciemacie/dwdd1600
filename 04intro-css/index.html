Brief Overview

Porcupine Tree are unquestionably one of the most difficult-to-categorize and innovative bands out there. The music is breathtaking, ranging from psychedelic trip-hop to progressive metal, and a compendium of other sounds in between.

The band is fronted by Steven Wilson, who also is well-known for his work producing other artists; from Swedish progressive metal group Opeth, to Norwegian chanteuse Anja Garbarek. He also has several other projects, including No-Man, Bass Communion, and the highly acclaimed collaboration with Israeli musician Aviv Geffen, Blackfield. Porcupine Tree was founded as a self-indulgent creative outlet for Wilson, and the first major release was On the Sunday of Life… in 1992, an album of psychedelia and studio experiments which bears little relation to the band's current sound. From here, Wilson expanded the sound, creating the progressive rock/ambient trance fusion on the 30-minute long single Voyage 34. One of the only constants in Porcupine Tree's music is how it continues to evolve and confront the expectations of the band's fans from album to album.

Members

Porcupine Tree's band members have stayed relatively consistent over the years. However, there have been guest appearances often coinciding with tours, and a rotation of new members over the 30 odd years the band has been together.

The core original band members are as follows:

  1. Steven Wilson, vocals, guitar. (1993-present).
  2. Colin Edwin, bass. (1993-2021).
  3. Chris Maitland, drums. (1993-2002).
  4. Richard Barbieri, keyboard. (1993-present).

Other talents that have contributed to Porcupine Tree:

  • Alex Lifeson of Rush, featured in Anesthetize.
  • Robert Fripp of King Crimson, featured in Way Out of Here.
  • Adrian Belew of King Crimson, featured in Deadwing and Halo.
  • Mikael Akerfeldt of Opeth, featured in Lazarus, Deadwing, and Arriving Somewhere But Not Here.
  • Aviv Geffen, featured in The Sound of Muzak, and Prodigal.
  • John Wesley
  • Suzanne Barbieri, featured in Up the Downstair, and The Sky Moves Sideways Phase 2.
  • Randy McStine, Nathan Navarro, touring for the 2022 Closure/Continuation tour.

Current lineup members consist of Steven Wilson, Richard Barbieri, and Gavin Harrison.

Record Labels

Porcupine Tree is not exclusively tied to one specific label, Delerium Records, Lava/Atlantic Records, Roadrunner Records, Music for Nations/Sony, and Transmission Records, to name a few. However, their current label as of their most recent album release Closure/Continuation, Porcupine Tree has been signed to Kscope since the late 1990s, to present day.
